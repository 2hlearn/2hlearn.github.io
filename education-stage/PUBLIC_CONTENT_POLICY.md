# Public teaching introductions

Use only a short public introduction: confirmed audience, at most two short outcomes, and two or three brief topic names. Hide unconfirmed fields. The preparing LLM introduction currently has only its supplied title, subtitle and preparation state.

Do not include day-by-day schedules, exercise sequences, assignments, code, teaching notes, detailed implementation recipes, original documents, or their download links. The course model intentionally has no free-form body field. Raw HWP/HWPX, PDF, Office documents and archives are rejected from this adapter source tree at build time.

Draft means excluded from the rendered site. It does not make content private in a public Git repository. Do not store sensitive originals as drafts, article examples, attachments, or reference files. The same editorial rule applies to articles. Field limits and build checks assist review; they do not replace human content review or detect every possible disclosure.

Never delete remote history or force push as an automatic remediation. Report an identified exposure and its exact file or URL before proposing cleanup.
